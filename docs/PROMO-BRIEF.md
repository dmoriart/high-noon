# Josh’s launch-image and gameplay-clip brief

Prepare after the first asset pack and playtest review. Use the game’s current approved title consistently; High Noon remains the repository/HUD working title and Crossfire is the present Studio place name. Des should settle the public title before Josh finishes lettering.

## Icon and thumbnail

Josh makes one original six-shooter silhouette/close-up against the frontier street, readable at a small size. For the thumbnail, show two actual-game cowboys using the real street cover; keep text short and the weapon/characters easy to identify. Start from a real build capture. No horses, open world, bounty mechanic or finished character art in the promotion until those features/assets actually exist. Do not use Red Dead Redemption characters, logos or copied artwork.

Draft description for Des to review:

“Grab a six-shooter and challenge your friends in a frontier-town shootout. Practise your aim while you wait, use the street’s cover, and race to ten eliminations. An early playtest built by a father and son—your feedback helps shape the next round.”

## Short gameplay clip

Capture 15–20 seconds from the reviewed build: 3 seconds of target practice, 5 of moving into a street gunfight, 4 of Josh’s gun firing/reloading and 3 of the result/replay. Use actual capture and original/licensed sound. Save a clean version without lettering for Josh’s portfolio. Make greybox status visible when unfinished art is in frame.

Compare honest thumbnails only after there are enough impressions to learn something. Record which image was active and examine qualified play and return visits; do not treat clicks alone as success. No advertising spend is authorised by this brief.
